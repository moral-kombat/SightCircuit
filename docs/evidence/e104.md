Device report, 2026-11-26 06:49 EST, recorded by the coordinator from the
Frame project manager's update check of that time. Nothing in this record was
verified from the cloud. The check also covers a scheduled check of
2026-11-25 18:48 that sat unread for about 12 hours. Nothing was run,
restarted or pulled on the Frame for it. It reports three changes since

camera was opened and nothing was displayed.

## What the Frame manager reported

**Baseline.** The baseline recorder said "baseline unchanged" before and
after: the same boot (419b6a79), SteamOS 0.5.10 build 20261118.6185655, up
1 day 10 hours, the Camera Switch directory `b70c3ebb…84e9`, Python 3.14.3,
the charger online and the battery at 100%.

**The tracking service crashed on 2026-11-25 at 07:06 EST.** At 07:06:04
CameraControl logged "Color passthrough requested: 1" and "Passthrough
cameras resumed". XRService at once ran low on queued buffers on all four
cameras, restarted `slam_right` and `slam_left` repeatedly, and ended with
"FATAL ERROR: [DeckardCaptureSource] Davis camera slam_right has been
restarted 4 times in the last 60 seconds. Exiting." It aborted (SIGABRT, core
of pid 11475 at 07:06:07, dumped 07:06:17). `vrserver` restarted it ("Service
did not exit gracefully"); the new XRService is pid 126017 from 07:06:10,
version 2.19.4. `vrserver` (11411), `vrcompositor` (11483), gamescope and
Steam are the processes of 2026-11-24 20:28: there was no session restart and
no reboot. The headset was on the charger at 100%, the battery at 22.5 °C.
**No SightCircuit process was running**; the Frame manager's previous check
had ended about 18 minutes earlier.

**The camera provider's socket is gone.** Since 07:06:12 CameraControl (pid
11597, still running) has logged "Color passthrough requested: 0",

`/tmp/av1_external_camera_provider.sock` no longer exists and nothing listens
on it; XRService listens on `@passthrough_camera`. There has been no colour
request since. The service's counters: colour connects 3 and disconnects 2
(they were 2 and 2); iova 0, ICP IRQ 0, suspend exits 0, IPE 0, failed
recoveries 0, ownership 2.

**An operating-system update is offered.** `atomupd-manager` reports build
20261125.6132624, version 0.5.11, variant `vr`, branch `beta`, about 2.45 GB.

**The checkout.** On `main`, clean, held at c525e02 for

## What follows

- The listener condition for L-D1's two runs is not met while the socket is
  absent. What brings it back is not known: the Frame manager will ask the
  owner what they see when the headset is next put on, and restarts nothing
  without the owner.
- The crash happened outside every device step (the last one is
  [E103](e103.md), the evening of 2026-11-24), so the passthrough rule's stop
  of the camera track does not apply. It is a second kind of instability
  beside the session crashes of [E102](e102.md) that happens with nothing of
  SightCircuit's running, which matters for attributing any later change
  during a run.

**The owner's decisions, 2026-11-26, in the coordinator's session.** Asked
whether the Frame should stay on 0.5.10 until the positions and removal runs
are done and take 0.5.11 afterwards: "Yes, runs first, update after
(Recommended)". Asked whether the Frame's checkout should be updated and the
camera helper rebuilt before those two runs, so that they use the Start hold
of work-order item L18: "Yes, update and rebuild first (Recommended)". Both
were relayed verbatim to the Frame manager, which confirms each step with the
owner in its own session.

**A fact from the code, for the pull.** The Frame manager asked whether a
helper built before the export notice changed (#116) would still be accepted
at 074134c. By the code it would: a run without display compares only the
protocol version, which is still 2, and no contract digest. The rule that
came with the owner's change of the notice stands all the same: the helper is
rebuilt before its next use on a checkout that has the change, because the
old binary would record the old sentence while the window shows the new one.
