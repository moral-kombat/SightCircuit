## Interpretation

The observer's scene precondition
(`sightcircuit/hud/observation.py`: threshold the grey mirror frame at 180,
count every contour, reject above `MAX_CONTOURS = 2048`) was tuned on
synthetic frames and had never seen a real mirror frame. It counts every
bright blob, including single-pixel noise, before the 25 px area filter that
the quad search applies. A passthrough camera image of a room, even a dim
wall, can carry thousands of such blobs from sensor noise, texture and
exposure. The mirror is also composited, so on-screen content could add
edges; what the two rejected frames contained is unknown, because the
observer saves no images by design.

This is the fail-closed precondition working as written, not a device
failure, and not the V4 tag/bit mismatch of [E75](e075.md). It says nothing
about the HUD mapping. **G3 stays open.** A further attempt needs a change to
the precondition, reviewed offline with diagnostics, and a new approval.
