# E102: Camera probes on Frame after an OS update, two session crashes and a power loss, both probes clean

[Status and compatibility anchor](../status.md#e102-camera-probes-on-frame-after-an-os-update-two-session-crashes-and-a-power-loss-both-probes-clean)

Device report, 2026-11-24 18:33 to 21:58 EST, recorded by the coordinator
from the Frame project manager's reports of that evening. Nothing in this
record was verified from the cloud. It covers the device's state before the
scan of [E103](e103.md): two SteamVR session crashes and a reboot with no
SightCircuit process running, a power loss while SightCircuit's offline tests
ran, the no-camera checks on SteamOS 0.5.10, and the two probes of
[the probe guide](../guides/camera-probe.md), Phase A and Phase B, against a
camera service that had changed since [E100](e100.md).

**Approval.** In the planner's session on 2026-11-24 the owner chose, after
the first crash, "Probe first, then scan (Recommended)"; after the reboot,
"Yes, approve on 0.5.10 (Recommended)"; and, told of the power loss and the
second crash with a recommendation to wait, "No, proceed tonight". When that
last question was put, the planner had told the owner that nothing of
SightCircuit's was running at the power loss; that was wrong, was corrected
to the owner within the hour, and the owner did not change the decision.
Three Frame manager sessions were lost or replaced during the evening (one
archived, one lost with the power, one taking over); the owner confirmed each
new one in the planner's session before any relay went to it. In the Frame
manager's session the owner confirmed each step: step 0 "Yes, once load
settles (Recommended)"; the connect check "Yes, run it once" and, on the
helper's notice, "Yes, I've read it; run it"; the metadata check "Yes, run it
once" and again "Yes, I've read it; run it". The Frame manager typed the
confirmation through tmux on those answers, as in [E95](e095.md) and
[E96](e096.md).

**Outcome in one line.** On SteamOS 0.5.10, with a newer Arcturus and Camera
Switch than the helper had met, the helper connected, read the control state
and ten frame sets of metadata, and left cleanly both times with passthrough
unchanged; the format, the rate and the calibration digest are those of
[E97](e097.md), and one control field differs.

## Limits

A device report relayed by peer sessions, recorded as received. It shows that
the helper built on 2026-11-18 still connects to and reads metadata from the
camera service of Arcturus 26826366 and Camera Switch `b70c3ebb…` on SteamOS
0.5.10, in the arrangement where CameraControl hosts the provider, with the
format and rate unchanged. It does not cover the provider embedded in the
Arcturus app while the app is open. The cause of neither session crash is
known; the pattern of two crashes after two closes of the Arcturus app is an
observation, and no SightCircuit process ran in either. The cause of the
power loss is not known: SightCircuit's offline tests were running on
battery at the time, which is why they are now run on the charger, one
module at a time, on two cores. Why `resolution_mode` changed and why the
host-clock offset is smaller are not known; the helper wrote no control. The
red LED was not checked. Every gate in `sightcircuit/gates.py` stays closed.
