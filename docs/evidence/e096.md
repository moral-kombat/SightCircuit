## Limits

A device report relayed by a peer session, recorded as received. It shows
that the SDK-linked helper, built from the pinned release, connects to the
camera service on the Frame, completes the handshake, reads two cameras'
existence, the calibration's size and digest, and the shared control state,
and disconnects cleanly, without a wearer-visible change to passthrough. It
shows nothing about frames: the reader was never activated, so the pixel
format, modifier (linear or UBWC), plane layout, timestamps and standby
behaviour are Phase B's questions. The control state says the cameras run at
60 frames a second, 8-bit, sRGB transfer, BT.709 primaries and BT.601
encoding; those are configured values, not measured frames. The starting
state with external ownership held and colour passthrough is recorded as
observed; what it means for the passthrough rule in later phases is the
owner's to judge with this record. No disturbance was reported, so the SDK
track continues; Phase B (S5) needs its own approval.
