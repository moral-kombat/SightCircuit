## Reading

- The left camera's image, as the helper delivers it, is a quarter turn from
  upright. E100 ran the detector on the unturned whole frame, and here that
  entry found nothing with either detector; so E100's miss is explained by
  orientation. The cloud check in E100 had also shown that a turned frame
  hides a bottle; its other suggestion, that the bottle was too small, is not
  supported: the bottle is large in the image.
- Between the two quarter turns the lean is to 90 degrees, strongly for
  `yolox-nano` (23 of 38 against 3 of 37) and less so for `mobilenet-ssd`
  (17 of 38 against 9 of 37). A scan gives the axis and not the sense with
  certainty; the `positions` run settles it.
- For the runs that follow, the region is the centre half and the rotation
  90: `--region 0.25,0.25,0.75,0.75 --rotation 90`. With that view the scan's
  own frames were 7 of 7 for both detectors, which is seven frames, not a
  coverage figure.
