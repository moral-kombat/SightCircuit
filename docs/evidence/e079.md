## Log analysis (read-only, on the saved bundle)

- Every hash in the bundle's manifest verified. The running versions were
  confirmed from live processes, not from installed manifests.
- The 09:36 wake on 2026-11-01 had a fault burst: failures started 1.7 s after
  waking and lasted about 27 s. It ended when the camera firmware reloaded,
  about 7.6 s after the colour cameras returned.
- That recovery has the same shape as the one after V4 on 2026-10-24
  (22:50:22 to 22:50:28): external camera ownership is requested, Room View
  switches to the tracking cameras for about 4.5 to 5 s, then ownership is
  released and the colour cameras reconnect. At 09:36 the Camera Switch
  settings file was written just before the release, consistent with someone
  reacting on waking. The logs cannot show what the view looked like.
- In the old boot, 7 of 15 colour reconnects were followed by ICP failures
  within about 0.2 s. A switch back to colour can start a burst.
- Since the 2026-11-01 reboot: no bursts, and one routine failure that
  recovered at once.
- **The logs do not explain the green input.** Its cause stays unknown.

## Recovery procedure and rehearsal

The procedure, written from E59, E75 and the [lifecycle review](../lifecycle-review.md):
switch to the grey tracking view in Camera Switch; wait at least 10 s; switch
back to colour and watch for 30 s; if green persists or returns, switch back
to grey, stop and take the headset off, with no SteamVR restart and no
reboot.

Rehearsal, evening of 2026-11-03, the owner wearing the headset, the Frame
session following the XRService and kernel journals read-only:

- The owner switched to grey and back to colour once.
- The logs matched each step. There were no camera failures and no faults.
- Passthrough and tracking stayed normal throughout and for 30 s after.
- No green input appeared.
- The only camera event all evening was a routine failure on wake from
  standby at 21:56, recovered on the first retry.

**G2 is closed on the owner's confirmation, under the
[owner decision of 2026-11-03](../poc-scope.md#owner-decisions-2026-11-03):**
the cause is unknown, and the rehearsed procedure stands in for a root cause.

## Limits

- A rehearsal on a headset that is not green shows that the procedure is safe
  to run. It does not show that it cures green input.
- Nothing here identifies which process produced the ownership log lines, or
  why the input turned green on 2026-10-24.
- Physical recovery is the owner's report; the logs establish process and
  camera state only.
